Business Requirements Document (BRD)
Subscription Management System Enhancement

1. Executive Summary
This document outlines the business requirements for enhancing the subscription management system to provide better user experience and improved business analytics.

2. Business Objectives
- Improve subscription upgrade/downgrade flow
- Reduce involuntary churn through better retention features
- Provide comprehensive analytics for business decision making
- Ensure compliance with payment processing standards

3. Functional Requirements

3.1 Subscription Plan Management
- Users must be able to view current subscription details
- Users must be able to upgrade or downgrade their plans
- System must handle prorated billing automatically
- Payment methods must be securely stored and processed

3.2 Cancellation Process
- Self-service cancellation option must be available
- Retention offers should be presented before final cancellation
- Cancellation must include confirmation step
- System must handle end-of-period cancellations

3.3 Analytics and Reporting
- Real-time dashboard with key subscription metrics
- Churn analysis and prediction capabilities
- Revenue reporting by plan and time period
- User segmentation and cohort analysis

4. Non-Functional Requirements
- System must handle 10,000 concurrent users
- Payment processing must be PCI DSS compliant
- Data must be backed up daily
- System availability must be 99.9% uptime

5. Success Criteria
- 20% reduction in support tickets related to subscription management
- 15% improvement in customer retention rate
- 95% user satisfaction score for subscription processes
